An overview of keys you can press

# Playing
## Overworld
| Key(s)            | Description
|:-----------------:|:-------------------------:
| W or Arrow up     | Makes you walk up (alternative to walkpad)
| A or Arrow left   | Makes you walk left (alternative to walkpad)
| S or Arrow down   | Makes you walk down (alternative to walkpad)
| D or Arrow right  | Makes you walk right (alternative to walkpad)
| Space             | Action button / interact, including: talking to NPCs, opening chests
| Q                 | Leave Game
| E                 | Inventory
| F                 | Emergency button if you got stuck, type SPIT to teleport to a safe place

## Credits
| Key(s)            | Description
|:-----------------:|:-------------------------:
| Space             | Speed up (x9)
| Arrow right       | Go back

## Map Maker
| Key(s)            | Description
|:-----------------:|:-------------------------:
| W or Arrow up     | Makes you walk up (alternative to walkpad)
| A or Arrow left   | Makes you walk left (alternative to walkpad)
| S or Arrow down   | Makes you walk down (alternative to walkpad)
| D or Arrow right  | Makes you walk right (alternative to walkpad)
|
| Q                 | Cycle between layers (BG, BG2, FG)
| U                 | Undo
| I                 | Redo
| O                 | Copy tile
| P                 | Paste tile
| L                 | Toggle Autolayer setting
| F                 | Toggle fill
| Z                 | Zoom
|
| X                 | Switch to mode: Move
| C                 | Switch to mode: Move and Place
| V                 | Switch to mode: Erase
| B                 | Switch to mode: Tile
| 1 - 9             | Select some of the recent prepicked tiles
| 
| E                 | Open Tile Picker
| N                 | Toggle Save/Load buttons
| M                 | Open Map Info
