An overview of keys you can press

# Playing
## Overworld
| Key(s)            | Description
|:-----------------:|:-------------------------:
| W or Arrow up     | Makes you walk up (alternative to walkpad)
| A or Arrow left   | Makes you walk left (alternative to walkpad)
| S or Arrow down   | Makes you walk down (alternative to walkpad)
| D or Arrow right  | Makes you walk right (alternative to walkpad)
| Space             | Action button / interact, including: talking to NPCs, opening chests
| Q                 | Leave Game
| E                 | Inventory
| F                 | Emergency button if you got stuck, type SPIT to teleport to a safe place

## Credits
| Key(s)            | Description
|:-----------------:|:-------------------------:
| Space             | Speed up (x9)
| Arrow right       | Go back
